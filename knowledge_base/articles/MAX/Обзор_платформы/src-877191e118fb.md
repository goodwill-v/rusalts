"Digital ID" integrated into AAM Systems solutions AAM Systems has implemented support for the "Digital ID" service in its software suites APACS and LyriX . The company announced this on June 15, 2026. "Digital ID" is a service based on the Russian messenger MAX, designed for storing electronic documents and verifying user identity. Thanks to integration with AAM Systems' solutions, the digital identifier can be used as a full-fledged pass in access control and management systems. As part of the integration , the APACS and LyriX software suites interact with the MAX messenger to transmit and verify data regarding passes. The system receives and processes the user's identifier, after which it makes a decision on granting access to the facility. To support this access mechanism, AAM Systems developed the universal AAM MAX reader. The device supports operation with both the "Digital ID" service and popular RFID card formats, including EM-Marine, MIFARE, DESFire, and HID Prox. The built-in screen provides user feedback at access points. According to the company, using this technology helps reduce the risks of using counterfeit or third-party transferred passes, while also improving access convenience through digital identification tools.
