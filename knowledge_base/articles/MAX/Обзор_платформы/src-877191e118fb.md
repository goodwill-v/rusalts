Alerting Users on Suspicious Links MAH started alerting users when they click on suspicious links. The messenger's press office announced this on October 13, 2026. MAH uses a dual-layer protection system — every link click is verified in real time, and users targeted by fraudsters automatically get Safe Mode enabled without any manual configuration. When a user clicks a link in the messenger , automated systems scan it for threats. If the link is flagged as dangerous, a pop-up notification appears. Additionally, Safe Mode is automatically enabled for 24 hours for users targeted by a fraudster. Only contacts from the address book can call or add them to chats. Users can disable it manually in the messenger settings. The user receives a notification in the MAH bot and can manage the setting manually in the Security section. After 24 hours, the mode turns off automatically if no changes were made. Шаблон:Цитата Russia Allows Drivers to Present Licenses and VRC via Max Russian_Government revised the rules for presenting driver's licenses and vehicle registration documents. Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin signed the corresponding decree on August 28, 2026. The regulation legalizes the use of digital codes in the Max messenger as an alternative to paper originals when verifying driving privileges and vehicle registration data. Under the decree, the driver's license and vehicle registration certificate (VRC) in the Max app will be displayed as two-dimensional barcodes. The feature is implemented within the Digital ID service, which allows users to store electronic copies of key documents (passport, tax ID, SNILS, OMS policy). The Ministry of Digital Development announced that drivers will be able to present documents to GAI officers within the coming months. 𝓲 Фото: Generated by Gemini neural network Drivers in Russia can now show their licenses and VRC via Max A similar option is available in the Gosuslugi app, where users can generate a QR code for their license. The Digital ID service in Max also allows users to present electronic document versions for other purposes. Expanding Digital ID Creation Capabilities MAX has expanded the capabilities for creating a Digital ID. The company’s press service announced this on August 5, 2026. 𝓲 Фото: VK Users aged 14 and over can now register for a Digital ID, which serves as a digital equivalent of paper documents within MAX. Teenagers will be able to use the Digital ID to verify their age and social status at cinemas, museums, and for transport discounts. Initially, the ability to create a Digital ID will be available to students and individuals with disabilities aged 14 and older. To do so, users must have a verified account on the State Services portal and complete a few simple steps in MAX: open the "Digital ID" section in the messenger, click the "Create QR" button, provide consent for personal data processing, set a PIN code for login. After registering the Digital ID, the user's profile card will display their student status, grade or year of study, and the name of the educational institution. Hotel check-ins and purchases of 18+ products using the Digital ID are only permitted upon reaching the corresponding age.
