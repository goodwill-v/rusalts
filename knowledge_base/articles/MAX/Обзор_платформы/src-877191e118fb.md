Шаблон:Цитата Russia Allows Drivers to Present Licenses and VRC via Max Russian_Government revised the rules for presenting driver's licenses and vehicle registration documents. Prime Minister Mikhail Mishustin signed the corresponding decree on August 28, 2026. The regulation legalizes the use of digital codes in the Max messenger as an alternative to paper originals when verifying driving privileges and vehicle registration data. Under the decree, the driver's license and vehicle registration certificate (VRC) in the Max app will be displayed as two-dimensional barcodes. The feature is implemented within the Digital ID service, which allows users to store electronic copies of key documents (passport, tax ID, SNILS, OMS policy). The Ministry of Digital Development announced that drivers will be able to present documents to GAI officers within the coming months. 𝓲 Фото: Generated by Gemini neural network Drivers in Russia can now show their licenses and VRC via Max A similar option is available in the Gosuslugi app, where users can generate a QR code for their license. An officer may accept such a code by scanning it with a service tablet to verify it against the departmental database. However, as Alexander Kholodov, a member of the Public Chamber of Russia , explained, the technical ability to display an electronic version does not eliminate the legal obligation to carry the paper original. The Digital ID service in Max also allows users to present electronic document versions for other purposes. To use the feature, users must authenticate through the Gosuslugi portal and consent to personal data processing, after which QR code cards are generated in their profile. This feature is used to verify age when purchasing tobacco or alcohol, as well as to confirm eligibility for benefits. Expanding Digital ID Creation Capabilities MAX has expanded the capabilities for creating a Digital ID. The company’s press service announced this on August 5, 2026. 𝓲 Фото: VK Users aged 14 and over can now register for a Digital ID, which serves as a digital equivalent of paper documents within MAX. Teenagers will be able to use the Digital ID to verify their age and social status at cinemas, museums, and for transport discounts. Initially, the ability to create a Digital ID will be available to students and individuals with disabilities aged 14 and older. To do so, users must have a verified account on the State Services portal and complete a few simple steps in MAX: open the "Digital ID" section in the messenger, click the "Create QR" button, provide consent for personal data processing, set a PIN code for login. After registering the Digital ID, the user's profile card will display their student status, grade or year of study, and the name of the educational institution. Hotel check-ins and purchases of 18+ products using the Digital ID are only permitted upon reaching the corresponding age. The operators of the "Big Four" and the platform's management signed an agreement that establishes zero-rated traffic for all users on these networks. The agreement covers voice calls, text messages, file transfers, and interaction with digital services within the app. Using Max will not be charged and will not deduct data from package volumes internet . Users will get unlimited access to messenger features regardless of their remaining gigabytes or account balance status. 𝓲 Фото: Yevgeny Razumny/Kommersant Messenger Max canceled fees for mobile traffic CEO of Beeline Sergey Anokhin noted that his company joined the initiative because Max is gaining popularity not only as a communication tool but also as a service platform. CEO of Megafon Khachatur Pombukhchan confirmed the operators' intention to combine resources to create a unified information environment.
